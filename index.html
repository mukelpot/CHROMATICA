Synopsis

You're not buying a ticket. You're buying an experience.

DYAC – CHROMATICA: A Spectrum of Harmonies is the annual production held by SMU VOIX for the 2025–2026 cycle. This large-scale production will feature a lineup of performances from the different groups of VOIX members, showcasing their months of vocal and choreography practice.

CHROMATICA celebrates the beauty of individuality and the power of coming together. Just as a rainbow is formed from many distinct colours, each person carries their own unique voice, story, and perspective. Alone, these differences make us who we are; together, they create something far richer and more vibrant.

In a cappella, every voice serves a different purpose. Some carry the melody, while others build harmony, rhythm, or texture. While each part is unique, it is only when they come together that music truly comes to life. CHROMATICA reflects this idea: a spectrum of voices, personalities, and experiences woven together into one cohesive whole.

Through four acts, the concert explores the many colours that make up our collective story: moments of joy and connection, vulnerability and reflection, confidence and self-expression, and ultimately acceptance and peace. Though each shade is distinct, together they form a spectrum of harmonies that is greater than the sum of its parts.

About SMU VOIX

SMU VOIX is an a cappella group at Singapore Management University, redefining a cappella. Do You A Cappella is its annual production, where the different groups of VOIX members bring months of vocal and choreography practice to one stage.

Behind every show are months of effort: teamwork and dedication, creative brainstorming and late-night rehearsals, all for one radiant night.
